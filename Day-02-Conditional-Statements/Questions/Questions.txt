JAVA DAILY TASKS
DAY 02 - CONDITIONAL STATEMENTS
================================

Q01 - POSITIVE OR NEGATIVE
--------------------------
Get a number from the user and check whether the number is
positive, negative, or zero.


Q02 - ODD OR EVEN
-----------------
Get a number from the user and check whether it is odd or even.


Q03 - LARGEST OF TWO NUMBERS
----------------------------
Get two numbers from the user and find the largest number.


Q04 - SMALLEST OF TWO NUMBERS
-----------------------------
Get two numbers from the user and find the smallest number.


Q05 - LARGEST OF THREE NUMBERS
------------------------------
Get three numbers from the user and find the largest number.


Q06 - LEAP YEAR
---------------
Get a year from the user and check whether it is a leap year.


Q07 - STUDENT GRADE
-------------------
Get a student's mark and display the grade.

90 - 100  -> A
80 - 89   -> B
70 - 79   -> C
60 - 69   -> D
50 - 59   -> E
Below 50  -> Fail


Q08 - ELECTRICITY BILL
----------------------
Get electricity units from the user.

0 - 100 units     -> Rs.5 per unit
101 - 200 units   -> Rs.6 per unit
Above 200 units   -> Rs.8 per unit

Add 10% surcharge to the calculated bill.


Q09 - DISCOUNT CALCULATION
--------------------------
Get the purchase amount from the user.

Amount >= Rs.5000  -> 20% discount
Amount >= Rs.3000  -> 15% discount
Amount >= Rs.1000  -> 10% discount
Below Rs.1000      -> No discount

Display the discount amount and final amount.


Q10 - LOAN ELIGIBILITY
----------------------
Get the user's age and monthly salary.

Age >= 21
AND
Monthly salary >= Rs.30,000

If both conditions are satisfied, display:

Loan Eligible

Otherwise display:

Loan Not Eligible.
